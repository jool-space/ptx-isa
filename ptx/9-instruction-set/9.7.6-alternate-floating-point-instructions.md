### 9.7.6. Alternate Floating-Point Instructions


Alternate floating-point instructions operate on FP8, FP6, FP4 data types. The register variable to be used for such instruction varies depending upon the data type. Refer Packed Floating Point Data Types for details on the supported data types and their register representations.


The alternate floating-point instructions are:


  * `add`

  * `sub`

  * `mul`

  * `fma`
